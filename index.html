DEMONSTRATION PROJECT / 01

AI-Powered Lead Research & Qualification System

A structured research workflow designed to help a small digital marketing agency identify and qualify potential small-business prospects.

BUSINESS→RESEARCH→QUALIFICATION→CRM / SHEET→OUTREACH
01 / PROBLEM

Manual prospect research can take time and result in uneven records. This demonstration explores a consistent structure for collecting, verifying, and assessing business information.

02 / APPROACH

Define a repeatable sequence: set qualification criteria, research each business, verify information, assess fit, organize findings, and check the record before handoff.

03 / RESEARCH CRITERIA

Illustrative criteria include business category, service area, signs of an active business, available public contact channels, and relevance to the agency’s target audience. Criteria should be agreed before research starts.

04 / DATA STRUCTURE

Business name · Website · Category · Location · Public contact channel · Research source · Fit notes · Qualification status · Last verified date.

05 / QUALIFICATION LOGIC

Use clear, agreed rules to mark a prospect as a potential fit, needs review, or not a fit. Keep the reason visible so another person can understand the decision.

06 / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Check for duplicate records, broken or mismatched links, missing fields, unsupported assumptions, and stale information. Keep notes traceable to public sources.

07 / WHAT I LEARNED

The structure of the research matters as much as the research itself. Consistent definitions and visible sources make handoff and review more useful.

08 / FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS

Test criteria with a real brief, refine data fields with the team, document sourcing standards, and explore automation after the manual workflow is clear.

This is a demonstration project. It does not report client work or claim real-world leads, revenue, or results.
